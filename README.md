

A# 📊 Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

An executive, interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze retail sales performance, regional distribution, product category profitability, and customer purchasing patterns.

## 📌 Project Summary

* *Domain:* Retail & Sales Analytics
* *Tools Used:* Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Data Modeling, Dashboard Design)
* *Dataset:* Superstore Sales Dataset
* *Key Metric:* Total Sales of *$2,261,537*

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

* *Top Performing Cities:*
*New York City* leads all sales with approximately *$196,354*, followed by *Los Angeles* ($135,753) and *San Francisco* ($100,171), highlighting key metropolitan revenue hubs.
* *Category Breakdown:* Sales are well-balanced across segments, with *Technology* driving the largest share (*35%*), closely followed by *Furniture* (*33%*) and *Office Supplies* (*32%*).
* *Regional Contribution:* The *West* region dominates total revenue generation ($575,259), followed by the *East* ($543,804), while the *Central* and *South* regions represent opportunities for expansion.
* *Sales Growth Trajectory:* Annual sales demonstrated consistent year-over-year growth, accelerating significantly to hit peak revenue of *$722,052* in 2018.
* *Key Customers & Products:* A core group of high-value individual customers and top-tier technology hardware (such as copiers and telepresence systems) account for disproportionately high order volumes.

---

## ⚙️ Interactive Features

* *Dynamic Slicers:* Interactive multi-select filters for *Category*, *Region*, and *Customer Segment* to drill down into specific business dimensions.
* *Clean UI/UX:* Built with a custom card-based executive layout, soft contrast palette, removed gridlines, and streamlined chart headers for executive presentations.
